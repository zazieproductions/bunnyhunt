Who actually makes your bunny

Follow-up to the shopping card. This one is about the factory side — what I could prove, what I could only narrow down, and the message that closes the gap. Checked 1 November 2026.

The short answer first

There is no brand behind your bunny, so there is no name to look up. Every name you've seen on him — ihreesy, ARTIBETTER, Toyvian, Luxshiny, iLikeGift — is a seller's label stuck on someone else's mould. The actual maker is an unbranded resin workshop in one of China's two craft clusters.

I narrowed it to a shortlist of named companies, ruled out the biggest obvious candidate by photo comparison, and wrote you the exact enquiry that makes a factory reveal whether the mould is theirs. That last part is the part I can't do from here — it needs a message sent with his photo.

1. Why there's no "real brand" to find

A useful test: look at what else the brand sells. A factory sells one category. A relabeller sells everything.

3. Where it's made — narrowed to two clusters

4. The message that gets an honest answer

Send this with his photo attached (front-on, the version at the top of this page). Ask all four questions — the fourth is the one that separates a factory from a trader, because a trader physically cannot produce a mould photo.

English

Hello — I am looking for the factory that produces this exact rabbit figurine (photo attached). White glossy resin, 5×5×6.5 cm, sitting with both back legs splayed sideways, eyes closed, pink cheeks. It is one pose from a set of five poses.

  1. Do you produce this exact item, or can you supply it?
  2. Is this mould your own, or do you buy the finished item from another factory?
  3. What is your minimum order quantity and unit price for 2 / 10 / 100 pieces?
  4. Can you send me a photo of the mould itself, and confirm which of the five poses you can supply?

I only need a small quantity shipped to the United States. Thank you.

Chinese — better response rate on 1688 / Yiwu suppliers

你好，我想找生产这款兔子摆件的工厂（附图片）。白色亮面树脂，5×5×6.5厘米，后腿向两侧伸开坐着，闭眼，粉色腮红。它是一套五个造型中的其中一个。

  1. 这款是你们自己生产/有货吗？
  2. 这个模具是你们自己开模的吗？还是从别的厂拿货？
  3. 起订量和单价是多少（2个／10个／100个）？
  4. 能否拍一张模具照片给我？五个造型里你们能做哪几个？

我只需要少量，发到美国。谢谢。

How to read the replies: "we have this mould, here is the photo" = you found it. "we can make it, send deposit for new mould" = they don't have it and want to charge you for tooling. "this one is out of stock but we have similar" = trader. A factory that owns the mould usually answers with a photo and a carton quantity (e.g. 200/400/600 pcs per carton), because that's how they think.

5. What I'd do next, if you want to keep going

  1. Send 4–6 of these enquiries — two Yiwu, three Fujian. Most reply within a day or two. That's the step that converts this list into a name.
  2. Customs-record search. US import records (ImportYeti, Panjiva) show which Chinese supplier shipped a product to which US buyer. If any American importer ever brought these in, the factory name is in the record. Worth one pass if the enquiries come back vague.
  3. Re-check the two live doors from the shopping card (Amazon seller offers, the AliExpress IDs) — those get you a bunny now, while the factory hunt gets you a supply for later.
  4. Tell me what the suppliers reply and I'll read the answers with you and tell you which ones are bluffing — that's usually where people get talked into a 500-piece order they didn't want.
Method: Chinese-language wholesale search (1688 listings, 义乌购/yiwugo official Yiwu market platform, chinagoods), Made-in-China and Alibaba supplier directories, reverse-image search on the factory photo set, and direct photo comparison of every candidate mould. Company names, booth addresses, MOQs and prices are as published by those suppliers on 1 Nov 2026. Confidence labels are mine: "worth asking" = sells this exact category at the right scale, unverified mould; "second wave" = plausible but less specific. Nothing here has been verified by the supplier itself — that's what the enquiry is for.
