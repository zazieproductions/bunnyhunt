How to recognise him, exactly

  • Chubby white body, sitting flat with both back legs splayed out sideways.
  • Eyes closed in two happy downward curves, with small lashes at the outer corners.
  • Tiny red-orange nose and a small smiling mouth, plus two short whisker lines each side.
  • Rosy pink cheeks, and pink blush on the ear insides, paws and leg pads.
  • Glossy white resin. About 5 × 5 × 6.5 cm (2 × 2 × 2.5 in), roughly the size of a plum.

The product names you'll see for him: ihreesy "White Rabbit #E" (Amazon, MPN BQTK37157) · "Cute Rabbit Gift Box" resin micro-landscape ornament (AliExpress) · Temu goods ID 601099511750810.

He is one of five poses from a single factory set The other four are: paws together with eyes open, a winking one standing up, one lying down with eyes open, and one lying down with eyes closed. Sellers mix them up in photos constantly — so the photo in the listing is the thing to trust, not the title, and not the variant letter.

The 30-second check before you pay

Do this on any listing that claims to be him. If all five are yes, it's him:

  • Both back legs are sticking out sideways on the floor (not tucked, not standing).
  • Eyes are closed — two curved lines, no white eyeball showing.
  • Nose is red/orange and small, not black.
  • Cheeks have pink blush patches painted on.
  • It's a single figurine, glossy white resin, about 6.5 cm tall.

3 · Order the factory item through a shopping agent (Taobao / 1688)Most durable long-term fix

This figurine is a mass-produced Chinese factory item, which is exactly why it keeps appearing and disappearing. Buying it at the source is the one route that doesn't depend on a middleman's stock.

  • Use an agent that buys from Chinese sites and ships to the US: Superbuy, CSSBuy, or Sugargoo. You paste a product link, they buy it, then ship it to Asheville.
  • Search inside the agent with: 兔子摆件 微景观 (rabbit figurine, micro landscape), 车载兔子摆件 (car rabbit ornament), or 树脂兔子 摆件 睡觉 (resin rabbit figurine sleeping). Typical price at source: ¥3–8 each, so ordering two or three is cheap.
  • Alibaba also works: post a Request for Quotation for "resin sleeping rabbit figurine, 6.5 cm" and suppliers quote you; MOQs are often 2–10 pieces, and Trade Assurance protects the payment.

If every door is shut on the day you look

  1. Buy two when you find one. He's an end-of-line factory sculpt. One to replace, one to hold in reserve — that's the real fix for "it broke and there was nothing left".
  2. Keep the broken one. White resin glues back together very well: a gel cyanoacrylate (super glue gel) or two-part epoxy on the clean break, held still for a minute, then left overnight. A repaired bunny is still your bunny — and for a lot of people that matters more than a perfect one. If you want, I can write out exactly how to do the repair without making the break worse.
  3. Come back to this card. The AliExpress IDs and the Temu ID are the stable part — searches change, IDs don't. If you ask me again in a week or a month, I can re-check these same IDs and see what's moved.
