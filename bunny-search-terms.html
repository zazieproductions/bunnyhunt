The one-line version. The object has no brand, so no product name will ever find it. The five foreign words worth memorising anyway

Pose words you can combine freely with 兔子 摆件 when a seller's search is too narrow:

  • 趴 (pā) — lying belly-down, sprawled ← the key word
  • 睡 (shuì) — asleep
  • 闭眼 (bì yǎn) — eyes closed
  • 坐 (zuò) — sitting
  • 树脂 (shùzhī) — resin

5. The search that beats every word: the photo

No vocabulary will outperform image search, because the mould has no name. Feed a public URL to Yandex reverse-image (the only engine that has worked; Google Lens 500s, Bing silently degrades to text, TinEye is cookied out, Baidu and Sogou reject remote URLs).
